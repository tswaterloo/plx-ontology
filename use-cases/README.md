# Use cases

Each page has the full use case: what it covers, the questions it must answer and what a good answer looks like, the open questions for the business, what the model still needs, and what is out of scope.

1. [Quote to order](01-quote-to-order.md): Quote → customer PO → sales order in BC, or a lost quote.
2. [Procure to G/L](02-procure-to-gl.md): Supplier PO → receipt → invoice → G/L, for packaging and screening.
3. [Screening](03-screening.md): Cost and accounting for screened (printed) blanks.
4. [Packaging sourcing](04-packaging-sourcing.md): From a packaging quote to an approved part, PO, proforma invoice and deposit.
5. [Artwork and labels](05-artwork-lifecycle.md): Artwork and label approval, from proof to printed component to finished good.
6. [Brand requirements](06-brand-requirements.md): Customer brand requirements and the documents behind each claim.
7. [Expired material](07-expired-disposition.md): What happens to expired or short-dated lots: hold, extend, waive, write off or replace.
8. [Amazon FBA](08-amazon-fba.md): Replenishing Amazon: shipment, labels, customs, freight and the stock move.
9. [Equipment and assets](09-asset-tracker.md): Machines from purchase to production line, and whether each is capitalized.
10. [Grants](10-grants-program.md): Grant programs from eligible cost to claim to reimbursement.
11. [Operations maps (Ricardo)](11-ricardo-maps.md): Receiving, QC, release and complaints, traced through real examples.
