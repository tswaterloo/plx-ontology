# How to use the PLX Workbench

## 1. Open it

You can use the Workbench in your web browser or in the desktop app. Both show the same
pages, and you sign in the same way.

**In the browser.** Open the Workbench address your administrator gave you and choose
Sign in with Microsoft. Use your normal PLX work account.

**The desktop app (Windows and Mac).** Download the installer for your computer from the
Workbench address followed by `/desktop/`, open it, and follow the steps. The first time
the app starts it asks for the Workbench address once and remembers it. After that it
opens straight to your work, shows a notification when something is waiting on you, and
updates itself.

The installers are not signed yet. On Windows, if a blue "Windows protected your PC"
box appears, choose More info, then Run anyway. On a Mac, right-click the app, choose
Open, then Open again. You only do this once.

## 2. Find your way around

- **My work** is your list for today: mail waiting on you, late orders, alerts and
  anything an autopilot has prepared for you.
- **Ask** is the box at the top of every page. Type a question the way you would ask a
  colleague, or ask it to do something ("draft a reply to this supplier").
- **Help** has a short guide for your role, with example questions and a checklist for
  your first week. Pick your role the first time you open it.

## 3. Ask a question

1. Type the question, for example "Has the supplier confirmed PO 4512 and when does it
   ship?", and press Enter.
2. Read the answer. Each fact shows where it came from (Business Central, the Portal, an
   email or a document). Click a source to open it and check.
3. If something is missing, or you are not allowed to see it, the answer says so. It
   never fills a gap with a guess.
4. If the question could mean two things, the Workbench asks you which one you meant.

## 4. Approve a draft

When you ask the Workbench to do something, or an autopilot prepares work for you, you
get a draft. Nothing is sent or changed yet.

1. Open the draft. It shows exactly who it goes to and what it says or changes.
2. Edit it if you want.
3. Confirm it. Only then does it happen, and it is written down in the work log.

## 5. Autopilots

An autopilot prepares routine work for your role, such as a supplier's new ship date or
a customer purchase order. It is off until you turn it on for yourself on the Autopilot
page. While it is in Draft, it only prepares and you decide every time. Work about money,
prices, credit, permissions or new customers and suppliers always waits for a person.

## 6. What it will not do

- It will not change anything in Business Central, the Portal, Sage, email or Teams by
  itself.
- It will not send or approve anything without a person confirming it.
- It only shows what your permissions allow.

## 7. Something looks wrong?

Choose **Give feedback** in your account menu (in the desktop app, also Help > Give
feedback). Say what you asked, what you expected and what you saw; the page you were on
is added for you. Under an answer you can also mark it useful, not useful or wrong. The
**Feedback** page shows what you have sent and whether it has been fixed.
